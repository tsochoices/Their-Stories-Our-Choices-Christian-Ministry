OUR MISSION STATEMENT

Their Stories, Our Choices

“Their Stories, Our Choices” is a faith-based podcast ministry dedicated to sharing authentic testimonies, exploring life's decisions through a biblical lens, nurturing spiritual growth, and glorifying God through honest, uplifting, and Christ-centered conversations.

Our mission is to create a welcoming ministry platform where individuals can share their experiences, reflect on the choices that have shaped their lives, and discover how faith in God can bring hope, wisdom, healing, and transformation.

As a ministry first and a podcast second, we are committed to serving with humility, sharing truth in love, and using every opportunity to uplift others and point hearts toward God.

OUR FOUNDATION

Values That Guide Our Ministry

✝

Christian Values

We strive to reflect biblical principles, love, compassion, forgiveness, and faith in our words and actions.

♡

Respect & Integrity

We seek to treat every person with dignity, communicate honestly, and handle personal stories responsibly.

✦

Humility & Service

We approach each testimony with compassion and a willingness to learn, remembering that our purpose is to honor God.

OUR COMMITMENT

Words and Actions That Honor God

All parties participating in or representing this podcast ministry agree to conduct themselves in a manner that reflects Christian values, respect, integrity, humility, and a sincere desire to honor God through their words, conduct, and representation of the ministry.

We encourage thoughtful, uplifting conversations, respect for personal boundaries, and care when sharing the experiences of others. Every person who visits our ministry should be treated with dignity and compassion.
